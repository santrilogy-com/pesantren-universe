License

Free to use. Not free to re-sell.

Plain-language license for the free 1DesignTool 3D library: use the models in personal and commercial work, no attribution needed. Just don't resell them.

You can

  • · Use any model in personal and commercial projects: games, apps, websites, AR/VR, renders, animations, ads, client work.
  • · Modify it — remix the knobs, recolour it, cut it apart, merge it into your own scenes.
  • · Ship it inside your product, compiled into a game or bundled with an app.
  • · Use it without credit. A link back to 1designtool.com/3d is appreciated, never required.

You can't

  • · Re-sell or re-distribute the model files on their own, or bundled as a model pack, asset kit or library — modified or not.
  • · Upload them to another 3D marketplace or free-model site.
  • · Claim you made them, or use them to train a model that generates 3D assets.

The models are provided as-is, without warranty. They were generated by 1DesignTool's 3D template with a coding agent and checked by its design kit; they contain no third-party meshes, textures, logos or brand marks. Questions or a model you think shouldn't be here: feedback.stoicsoft.com.
